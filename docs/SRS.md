# 1. Introduction:
SR Arts is a signage and printing business located in Pandharpur.

Currently, orders are received offline through shop visits, phone calls, WhatsApp, Instagram, and YouTube.

The objective of this project is to build a complete online ordering and business management system where customers can browse products, customize them, upload reference images, place orders, make payments, and track order status.

The application will also provide an administration panel to manage products, orders, customers, pricing, coupons, invoices, and reports.

Initially, the project will focus on backend development using Java and Spring Boot. A frontend web application and mobile application can be integrated in future versions.



# 2.Purpose

The purpose of this project is to digitize the business operations of SR Arts by providing an online platform for customers and an administration system for business management.

The system aims to simplify order management, improve customer experience, reduce manual work, and increase business reach across India.

# 3. Scope

The application will allow customers to browse products, customize products, upload artwork, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

Administrators will manage products, categories, customization options, orders, pricing, customers, coupons, reports, invoices, and business settings.

Future versions may include employee management, inventory management, supplier management, AI-powered design suggestions, and mobile applications.


# 4. Stakeholders

## Business Owner

SR Arts (Pandharpur)

Responsible for managing products, pricing, orders, customers, business reports, and overall application settings.

---

## Customers

Customers can browse products, customize products, upload reference images, place orders, make payments, track orders, and submit reviews.

---

## Employees (Future)

## Designers (Future)
